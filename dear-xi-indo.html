DEAR (Drop Everything And Read)

The Magic Library

by Jostein Gaarder and Klaus Hagerup

The Magic Library tells the story of Nils and Berit, who both lives in diffrent towns in Norway

Nils and Berit communicate with each other by a shared "letter book" which they take turn in writing, then mail to the other. A strange phenomenon happen with them when they realize that they've been seeing the same old lady that's really fond of books.

What I like about this book, is that it's divided into two section: the letter book section, and the normal section.

in the letter book section, the book is formateed as if we are reading the actual letter book. I think this is a very creative formatting for a book. and it also makes a lot of sense to the story.

I would highly recommend this book. It's very creative with it's story and even book formatting. I think the story could be enjoyed for people of all age groups (except for 0-3 years old)
