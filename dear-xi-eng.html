DEAR (Drop Everything And Read)

Border Line

by Dianne Wolfer

Border Line by dianne Wolfer is a book that tells the story of a teenage girl, Cassandra Falconer (Cassie).

Cassie has to try and adapt to sudden but impactful changes in her life, which also seems to happen simultaneously. She had just experienced puberty, and humiliated at school. Her father lost his job, and offered to take their family to move to Eucla, to make up for his new job.

I think this book is trying to convey that life could be difficult, especially for a teenage girl such as cassie, but if we do not keep trying to adapt and push, then we won't be able to see the end of the difficulty.

I think this is a well-written book and i would definitely recommend it to people
